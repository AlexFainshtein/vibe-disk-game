How to play:
  • Fling with your thumb from the dotted blue launchpad to launch the balls.
  • Score by hitting BOMBS — each detonation explodes nearby squares for 1, 2, or 3 points each. The bomb itself adds 1 point.
  • Each earned point adds 1 second to the timer.
  • Direct hits on squares only shrink them (3 → 2 → 1 → 0) — no points awarded.
  • Hitting a zero (green) square upgrades it back to a "1" and flings it upwards.
  • Don't let any square reach the bottom.
  • Clear a level for a +10 point bonus.
  • Note: countdown timer tops out at 20 sec.
